Dom · it follows your voice
How to use it
  1. Put your script in. Tap New, give it a title, then either tap Paste (it takes what you've copied, from an email, Notes or a message from Dom), type it, or tap Open a file to pick a .txt or .md from Files. Ask Dom to "write me an autocue script for ..." and it comes back ready to copy.
  2. Mark it up, if you like. A blank line starts a new paragraph. [pause] or any words in square brackets are notes to yourself: shown small, never read. **two stars** either side of a word underlines it to stress.
  3. Set it up for the camera. Prop the iPad or phone just below the lens. Reading line at Top, column about 60%, text big enough to read from where you sit. The time it'll take shows under the script.
  4. Tap Start. Allow the microphone the first time. It counts you in, 3, 2, 1, and then you just talk.
  5. Speak at your own pace. The words you've said go grey and the line moves up with you. Stop to think and it waits. Go off script and it holds your place, then picks you up when you come back to the words.
  6. If you need to, tap any word to jump there, use the arrows to go back or on a line, or Pause. The buttons fade while you speak; tap the screen to bring them back.
  7. Tap Done at the end. If you ticked Record, you'll be offered the video to save or share.
If it doesn't follow you: open it in Safari (not from a Home Screen icon), check Siri and Dictation are on in Settings, or switch to Steady scroll and set the speed. On the Mac: space starts and pauses, the arrows move a line, Esc stops.
